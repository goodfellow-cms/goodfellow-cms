The AI assistant can add openly licensed photos from Openverse where the media library has none that fit. Photos are saved to the media library with their credits in `content/media.json`, and pages list the credits of the photos they use. A checkbox in the AI panel turns it on or off, and `ai: { stockPhotos: false }` in the config turns it off for everyone.
