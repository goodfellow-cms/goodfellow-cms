Collections have five more kinds of field: yes or no, links to items in a collection (one or several, renamed with the items they link to, taken out when one is deleted, and checked before publishing), several images, a file, and repeating groups of a few fields. Entry field shows each, Collection list and loop can show only items where a yes or no field is on, and, in a page design, only the items the page's item links to. The AI assistant fills in yes or no fields and links to items.
