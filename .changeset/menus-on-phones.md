Menus work on phones. The Menu block's **Show the links behind a menu button (☰)** shows every link, every level of them, under a button on phones, on phones and small tablets, on phones and tablets, always or never, with no JavaScript; the editor says when a menu's submenus can't be reached on phones because it never shows the button. The starters' and the example's headers show it on phones and small tablets. In Next.js sites, the menu button's links close after moving to another page.

The admin panel works in narrow windows: its links, View site and the account are under a menu button there, and a Dark mode switch takes the place of the list of colors.
