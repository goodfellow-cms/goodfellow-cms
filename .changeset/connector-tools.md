The Claude connector can list drafts (`list_drafts`), move a page to a new address with its links and a redirect (`move_page`), and schedule pages and items with `showFrom` and `hideAfter` on `save_page` and `save_item`. Its message for a collection the site doesn't have now points to `get_site`, not a tool that doesn't exist.
