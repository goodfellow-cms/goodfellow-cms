The AI assistant's requests put the site's title, description, pages, collections, contact details, and images, and the page, collection, item kind, language, and links a task names, between the markers its instructions say hold the site's content, which asks the AI service to treat text an editor wrote there as content rather than as part of the request. It makes such text much less likely to steer the answer, though no AI service guarantees that, and answers are still checked against the site's fields.
