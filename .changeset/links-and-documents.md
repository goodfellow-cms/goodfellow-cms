Three built-in blocks: a Document viewer that shows a PDF from the media library on the page, a Donate button to a giving page such as a Stripe payment link, and Share buttons that link to share the page on social sites or by email. Donate and Share are links only, so pages load nothing from those services. WordPress imports bring PDFs WordPress shows on the page across as Document viewers, and exports write the Document viewer and Donate button as WordPress's own blocks.
