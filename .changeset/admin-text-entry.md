`@goodfellow-cms/admin/text` gives the admin panel's English with nothing to draw, for language packs' own checks: `goodfellowAdminTextSources()`, every string by its key, with Goodfellow's own blocks' labels (`BUILT_IN_LABELS`), and `languageStatus()` and `sourceState()`, which say what a language's file is missing, has out of date or can't use.
