Long text no longer slows down reading pages, themes, WordPress imports, feeds, or apps' themes: a comment, block comment, or `[caption]` that never ends, or a long run of slashes or hyphens, used to take time that grew with the square of its length. Comments, scripts, and styles that never end are now read as browsers read them, to the page's end. Early installs refuse package names and releases with characters npm's command line could read as more than a name.
