A Social post block shows posts from Bluesky, Mastodon, Instagram and X, and Spotify's and SoundCloud's players, from the post's address or its site's embed code, sandboxed and held back until visitors agree where the site asks. WordPress imports bring such embeds across as Social posts, and exports write them as WordPress's own embeds.
