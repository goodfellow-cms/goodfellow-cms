Move content between Goodfellow sites with nothing lost. **Export for another Goodfellow site** on Site Settings → Import, and `goodfellow export site`, download the same zip as Download a copy, which now includes `goodfellow-transfer.json`. **Import from another Goodfellow site**, and `goodfellow import site`, bring it into another site. They either add to its content, with a choice for each thing both sites have differently, or replace its content. The Site address, editors' limits, update settings and media storage always stay as they are. Blocks the export used from Goodfellow's block registry can be added in the same publish.
