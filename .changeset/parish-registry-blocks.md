Six more blocks in Goodfellow's registry, built with Tailwind alone: **News feed** (another site's RSS or Atom feed, read when the site is built), **Map** (a Google map of an address, or from Google Maps' embed code, with no API key), **Social links**, **Panel** (a box with a colored title bar), **Slideshow with quick links** (with a stop button, and still for visitors who ask for less motion) and **Content with section links** (a page's blocks beside the other pages in its part of the menu).

The Menu block has two more settings: **Colors**, which puts it in a bar of the theme's primary or secondary color with see-through dropdowns to match, and **Show the pages under each submenu link**, which opens a submenu link's own pages as a third level. Dropdowns near the menu's right end now open leftwards, so they stay on the page, and dropdowns set their own text color, so a color given to the menu doesn't make them hard to read.
