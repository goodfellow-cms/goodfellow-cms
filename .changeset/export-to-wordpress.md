Export a site to WordPress, as a WordPress export file that WordPress's importer reads: from Site Settings → WordPress, or with `goodfellow export wordpress` and `goodfellow-next export wordpress`. Pages keep their addresses and their blocks become WordPress's, items of collections with pages become posts with their categories and tags, and the menus and media library come too. The Import tab is now called WordPress.
