A Breadcrumbs block shows the way from the home page to the page shown, through the pages above it by address, such as Home › News › The parish fair, marked up as schema.org's `BreadcrumbList` for search engines. Items' pages come under their collection's page, pages blocks add after the page they're on, and sites in several languages start from the language's home page. It has a version for apps too. Core's `breadcrumbs()` works out the way from a list of pages.
