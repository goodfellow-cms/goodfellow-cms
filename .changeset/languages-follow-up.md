More for sites in several languages. Header & Footer picks a language and gives it a header or footer of its own. The page and item editors link to their translations, or make one. The site checks suggest translating pages and items that have no translation or an out-of-date one, when a publish brings them, and builds list them. Events and stories say their language in their structured data. In Next.js, the header and footer stay in the shared layout, one for each language, and the browser shows the page's.
