Sites in several languages. Add languages in Site Settings → Languages, each with the site's name and description in it; its pages go under its code, such as `/es/about`. The Pages screen and collections' lists translate pages and items into each language, say when a translation is out of date, and the AI assistant can translate a page or item. Each language can have menus of its own, the new Language switch block links to the page in each language, and pages say their language, link to their translations with `hreflang`, and are listed with them in the sitemap. Search finds only pages in the page's own language, and lists, feeds and calendars show only that language's items.
