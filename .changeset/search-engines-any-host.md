Pages can ask search engines not to follow their links or show their text, and choose a large or small picture when shared. Every page lets search engines show its pictures large, and link previews name the site, the page's language, the picture's description and size, and a dated item's date. Site Settings can ask AI companies' training crawlers to leave the site alone. Pages' full addresses (canonical links, the sitemap, feeds and the rest) now end with a slash, as most hosts serve the folders builds write, and so do links between pages, so following one needs no redirect; a site whose host serves them without one sets `trailingSlash: false` in its config, or Next.js's own. The docs say how to host a site anywhere, such as on AWS, Google Cloud or DigitalOcean, and the Domain screen explains connecting a domain on any host.
