Use fonts of your own: Colors & fonts' new Add a font uploads a font's WOFF2 files, checked in the browser, once the owner confirms its license allows websites, and the font menus offer it first. Pages load it from the site with `@font-face`, preloading the text's and headings' files, with size-matched stand-ins measured from the files, and the admin panel's editor and previews show it from its bytes.
